# Minimal Landing — usage

## Read Order

1. `USAGE.md`
2. `DESIGN.md`, then `../minimal/DESIGN.md` for anything it leaves to its parent.
3. `tokens.css` — copy it as the project's `src/styles/tokens.css` with `widgetarium system use minimal-landing`; change parameters, never formulas.
4. `references/`

## Design Highlights

- Minimal's colour and type, at a landing page's pace.
- One screen, one claim, one filled button.
- White space separates sections; no bands, no lines.
- A card proves a claim with a working piece of the product, a person or a price.
- Rows as cards for testimonials, steps and plans.
- One inverse plate closes the decision.

## Do

- Write the page as a sequence of claims, each with its proof, before choosing cards or rows.
- Use the product's own screens and the customers' own photos.

## Avoid

- A card with only an icon and a sentence.
- A second filled button on a screen.
- Gradient heroes, glows, 3D or drawn illustrations.
