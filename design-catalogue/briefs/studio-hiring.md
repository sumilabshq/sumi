# Brief: hiring tracker for a small studio

**For:** the two founders and one lead of a 12-person design studio who hire 1–3 people a quarter. Desktop.

**Tasks:**

- See open roles and how many candidates are at each step.
- Go through one role's candidates, move them between steps (applied, screen, task, interview, offer, hired, declined).
- Open a candidate: read their portfolio links, notes from each interview, the task result; leave a note; schedule the next step.
- Add a candidate or a role quickly.
- See who is waiting on the studio and for how long.

**Screens:** roles overview; one role's pipeline; a candidate's detail; the "waiting on us" list; settings for steps and people. Desktop only.

Invent the studio, roles, candidates and notes; keep them plausible.
