# Frosted: two test builds, 2026-10-11

Opus at low effort, a Claude Design canvas each, no component kit.

## 1. Fieldnotes, a research tracker — anchored, not clean

Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/8AisUAiVEAEViQWbdtPmPW

The prompt dictated the overview's composition ("pill navigation with one black item, light display
title, a giant figure, a full-width chart, tabs, a list with no row lines"), and the system then held a
panel-by-panel recipe of reference 1. The overview came out as reference 1 with the nouns changed and
was rejected as a copy. The other screens, described only by rules, were kept (refs 21, 22).

Lesson: the copy came from the prompt and the recipe, not from the pictures. "Page hero and layout"
was rewritten as principles.

## 2. A translator — clean

Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/1YoMmegWNpjdVmfzxFTCvj

The prompt gave only the product ("a language translator a person keeps open while they live, read
and talk in a second language"), the system with every reference, `clean-ui.md`, and two constraints:
no dashboard, and redesign any screen that reads like a reference with the nouns changed. The agent
chose the tasks and pages itself from DeepL, Google Translate, Reverso and phrasebooks. Approved; five
screens became refs 16–20.

Not fully unanchored: "no dashboard" and "4–6 artboards with a non-resting state" steered it.

## What the builds found in the system, fixed the same day

- Settings hold for the whole app; only `plates` differs, by page kind.
- Something opened over a page is a white card in both `plates` settings.
- A `reading` type step for the text a page exists for; short texts suit the system.
- The chosen option among alternatives is black, not lime.
- A line may split one screen into halves that face different people; air replaces lines in strips.
- A wash belongs to one plate; the ground never glows.
- The selection rule is for rows of choices; in a card grid the chosen tile turns white or washed.
