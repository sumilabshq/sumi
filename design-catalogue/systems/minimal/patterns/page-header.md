# Page header

What opens a page under the shell. Two ways this system does it; pick by the page's job, or design another in the same look.

## Status header (ref 5)

```
 Selling since May 2020                                [ Settings ]
 My listings
 3 buyers are waiting for your reply.
 ┌ Views 1,906 │ Saved 135 │ Waiting 3 │ Sold 23,400 ┐
 Active 7   Paused 2   Sold 11
```

An eyebrow, the display title, one sentence of live state in ink-2, small actions on the right, then optional figures and tabs with counts. For a page the person works from: a home, a queue, their own things.

## Hero header (ref 17)

```
 Deep dive                                   ‹ ›   ┌──────────────┐
 How to Design AI Citations                        │   picture    │
 A build playbook for grounded answers.            │              │
                                                   │              │
 Read deep dive →                                  └──────────────┘
```

An eyebrow, a heading, two or three lines of description and one text link at the bottom, beside a large picture. Arrows when it rotates through several items. For a page that leads with one thing to read or open: a home of articles, a featured item, a product's start page.

## Both

- One display title per page; the eyebrow is breadcrumbs, a date or context (`breadcrumbs.md`).
- A list, a record or a tool page usually needs less: a title and its tabs, or breadcrumbs and the record's name.
- Across the top of a site (ref 16): the product's name on the left, search and one tinted action on the right, nothing else.
